# Configure the connection

**Project configuration** (shared with your team):

* **Remote host**: your IBM i
* **Remote OBI path**: where OBI is installed on the IBM i
* **Remote ifs path**: the project folder on the IBM i

**User configuration** (personal):

* **User**: your IBM i user profile
* **SSH Key** or **Password**: a password is kept in the OS keychain, not in a file
